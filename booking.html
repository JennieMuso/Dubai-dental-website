Booking questions

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit?

No. The request is sent by email, and the clinic will contact you to confirm the date, time and appointment type.

How should I book dental pain or swelling?

Call the clinic so the team can ask about symptoms and arrange the earliest suitable appointment. Seek urgent medical care if swelling affects breathing or swallowing.

Can I request a particular dentist?

Yes. Add the clinician’s name in the message field. Availability and suitability will be confirmed by the clinic.

Can I change my appointment later?

Contact the clinic by phone or email as early as possible so the team can help find another time.
