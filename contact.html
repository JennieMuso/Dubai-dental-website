What should I do for severe dental pain or swelling?

Call the clinic promptly and explain the symptoms. If swelling affects breathing or swallowing, or you feel seriously unwell, seek urgent medical care.

Can I visit without an appointment?

Calling before you travel lets the team confirm availability and the most suitable appointment for your concern.

What should I bring to a first visit?

Bring identification, relevant insurance information, a list of medicines and any recent dental records or imaging you have been asked to provide.
