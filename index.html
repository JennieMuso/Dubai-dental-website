Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you visit our dental clinic in Downtown Dubai.

Ask another question →
How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from an examination every six months. Your dentist may recommend a different interval based on your oral health, gum health and treatment history.

Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

We reserve space for urgent concerns such as dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a lost filling. Call us so we can assess the urgency and arrange the earliest suitable appointment.

What happens at a first appointment?

Your first visit usually includes a conversation about your concerns and medical history, a dental examination, any clinically necessary imaging and a clear discussion of next steps and fees.

Can I consult a dentist before choosing treatment?

Yes. A consultation gives you time to explain your goals, understand suitable options and ask about timing, aftercare and costs before deciding whether to proceed.

Do you treat children and teenagers?

Yes. We welcome children and teenagers for preventive visits, dental care and age-appropriate guidance, with an emphasis on calm introductions and practical home care.

Where is Nacre Dental located?

We are on Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard in Downtown Dubai, UAE. Contact our team for the latest parking and arrival directions.
