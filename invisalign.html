What is Invisalign?

Invisalign moves teeth using a planned sequence of removable clear aligners.

Each aligner is worn according to your orthodontist’s schedule and applies controlled pressure to selected teeth. Digital records are used to plan movement, but in-person reviews remain important for monitoring fit, oral health and progress.

Could clear aligners suit you?

Invisalign can address many alignment and bite concerns, but suitability depends on the movement required and your ability to wear the aligners consistently.

Alignment concerns
Crowding, spacing and selected bite relationships may be suitable after orthodontic assessment.
Wear routine
Aligners are commonly worn around 20 to 22 hours a day and removed for eating and cleaning.
Oral health
Teeth and gums should be healthy before active orthodontic movement begins.
Complexity
Some movements may need attachments, elastics or a different orthodontic approach. Your clinician will explain the options.

Your aligner journey

Digital planning provides a map; reviews help make sure treatment continues to track as intended.

Consultation and scan

The orthodontist assesses your bite, oral health and goals, then records the teeth when aligners are suitable.

Review the digital plan

Wear and review

You change aligners on the prescribed schedule and attend reviews so fit, movement and oral health can be checked.

Retain the result

Retainers are planned after active treatment to help maintain the final tooth positions.

Invisalign questions

How many hours a day should aligners be worn?

Aligners are commonly prescribed for around 20 to 22 hours a day and removed for eating, drinking anything other than water and cleaning. Follow your orthodontist’s schedule.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Length depends on the movements required, wear consistency and biological response. A digital plan provides an estimated sequence after assessment.

Can I eat while wearing aligners?

Aligners are generally removed for meals and most drinks. Clean your teeth as advised before reinserting them to protect oral health and aligner clarity.

Will I need retainers afterwards?

Yes, retention is normally required after orthodontic treatment. Your clinician recommends the retainer type and wear schedule for your result.
