How we handle your information

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Nacre Dental receives through this website and how it is used when you contact us or request an appointment.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Information you provide

When you use the contact or appointment forms, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, preferred visit details and the message you choose to write. Please avoid including sensitive medical details in the website forms.

How website forms work

The forms prepare an email in your own email application. The website does not send the message to us automatically or store a submitted copy on this site. Your email provider may process the message under its own privacy terms. We use the details you send to respond to your enquiry, discuss an appointment and provide information you requested.

Website technology

This site may use essential browser features, local fonts and animation scripts to display pages and interactions. It does not intentionally use advertising trackers or sell information provided through the website.

Sharing and retention

Information you email to the clinic is available to staff who need it to respond or arrange care. We may retain correspondence as required for administration, clinical care or applicable legal obligations. We do not publish your personal information from a private enquiry.

Your choices and questions

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have sent, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact hello@nacredental.ae or call +971 4 555 0188. For detailed medical record and patient consent matters, speak directly with the clinic.
